Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and disclosed when you use our Discord bot (“Bot”) or participate in a Discord server where the Bot is installed or operated (“Server”).

Important: The Bot does not necessarily collect every category of information described below. Data collection depends on the features enabled on a particular server and the way users interact with the Bot.

1. Information We May Collect

1.1 Discord Account Information

When you interact with the Bot, we may process Discord-related identifiers and publicly available account information, including your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar information, server membership, and other information made available to the Bot through Discord's systems.

1.2 Server Information

For servers where the Bot is installed, we may process server or guild IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, server settings, and configuration information required to provide Bot functionality.

1.3 Commands and Interactions

We may process information about commands, buttons, menus, forms, tickets, or other interactions you make with the Bot. This may include the time of an interaction and the Discord account or server associated with it.

1.4 Messages and Message Content

Some Bot features may require access to message content or messages in specific channels. Where a feature requires this access, the Bot may process the relevant message content to provide that feature, moderate content, respond to commands, or perform other requested actions.

Message content is not necessarily stored permanently. Whether message content is retained depends on the particular feature and configuration of the Server.

1.5 Moderation and Security Information

If moderation features are enabled, the Bot may process information such as warnings, infractions, mutes, kicks, bans, moderation actions, appeals, reports, and related timestamps or moderator information.

1.6 Logs

The Bot may generate operational, security, moderation, or audit logs. Depending on the enabled features, these logs may contain Discord IDs, action information, timestamps, channel or server information, and other information necessary to understand an event.

1.7 Voice Features

If the Bot provides voice-related features, it may process information necessary to operate those features, such as voice-channel IDs and participation information. The Bot will only process audio or other voice data where the specific feature requires it and where permitted by applicable law and Discord's platform rules.

1.8 Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when using features such as support tickets, reports, applications, appeals, forms, or other Bot functionality. This information may include anything you choose to submit through those features.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and provide the Bot's features.
  • Store and apply server-specific settings and configurations.
  • Respond to commands and user interactions.
  • Provide moderation and security functionality.
  • Process reports, tickets, applications, and appeals.
  • Maintain logs and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, and unauthorized use.
  • Improve reliability, performance, and security.
  • Comply with legal obligations or valid legal requests.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, resolve disputes, maintain moderation records, or comply with legal obligations.

Different types of information may have different retention periods. For example, temporary operational data may be deleted sooner than moderation records or server configuration data.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services that are necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting providers, databases, logging services, APIs, or other infrastructure providers. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of the Bot, Server, users, or others.

5. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord's platform and may receive information made available through Discord's APIs and permissions. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policies.

We do not control Discord's collection or processing of information outside the information processed by our Bot and Server.

6. Server Administrators

Server administrators and moderators may configure features that determine what information the Bot can access or retain. They may also have access to moderation records, logs, reports, tickets, or other information generated by the Bot.

If you have a concern about information handled within a particular Discord server, you may also contact that server's administrators.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your data is processed.

You may contact the Bot's administrators to request deletion or information about data associated with your Discord account. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate security, moderation, legal, or operational reasons.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot and Server are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that information has been collected improperly from a child, please contact the Bot's administrators so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Cookies

The Bot itself does not use browser cookies. If the Bot is accompanied by a separate website or web dashboard, that website may use cookies or similar technologies as described in its applicable privacy notice.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published with a new “Last Updated” date.

12. Contact

If you have questions, privacy requests, or concerns about how the Bot processes information, contact the Bot's administrators through the official support method provided by the Bot or Server.

By using the Bot or participating in a Server where it operates, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.
